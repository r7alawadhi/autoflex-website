Why licensed matters

Every provider on Autoflex holds a valid UAE trade licence

Abu Dhabi Municipality fines car washing in parking lots and residential areas that aren't designated for it. Booking a licensed provider through Autoflex helps you stay within the rules.

  • Licence checked before listingWe onboard only businesses with a valid trade licence for the service they offer.
  • Clear prices before you bookEach provider sets their price and you see it in the app before you confirm.
  • Secure card paymentCard payments are processed by a licensed payment gateway. Autoflex never stores your card number.
