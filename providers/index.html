Questions

Common questions from providers

Does Autoflex employ my workers?

No. Autoflex is a booking platform. Your business stays independent, and you remain responsible for your team and the work they do.

How and when am I paid?

Customers pay by card in the app. Your share — the price minus the 10% commission — is paid to your business bank account on the schedule set out in your Service Provider Agreement.

What if a customer cancels at the last minute?

If a customer cancels less than 3 hours before the booked time, or you arrive and cannot reach or work on the car, the customer pays a fixed AED 15 fee and you receive AED 13.50 of it — the fee less the 10% commission.

Is there a minimum price?

Yes. Car wash services are listed at AED 35 or above, so the 10% commission still leaves the job worth doing. Above that, the price is yours to set.

Can I join without a trade licence?

No. Every provider on Autoflex must hold a valid UAE trade licence for the service offered. There are no exceptions.

Can I choose which bookings I take?

You set your working hours, days and coverage areas, so you only receive bookings that fit them. You then assign each job to one of your workers.
