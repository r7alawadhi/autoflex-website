Cancellations and refunds

These rules apply to bookings made and paid for in the Autoflex app.

  • Free cancellation: you can cancel a booking in the app free of charge up to 3 hours before the booked time.
  • Late cancellation or no access: if you cancel less than 3 hours before the booked time, or the provider arrives and cannot reach or work on your car, a fixed cancellation fee of AED 15 applies (never more than the booking value). The fee compensates the provider for the wasted trip, and the rest of your payment is refunded.
  • If the provider cancels or is late: if the provider cancels, does not arrive, or is more than 30 minutes late without agreeing a new time with you, you can cancel free of charge and receive a full refund.
  • How refunds are paid: refunds go back to the card you paid with. We process them within 5 working days; your bank may take 10 to 45 days to show the amount.

Problems with a service

If something is wrong with a service, tell us through the app, WhatsApp (+971 50 344 4224) or info@autoflex.ae, with photos if you can. Please tell us as soon as possible so we can act quickly:

  • Car wash: ideally within 24 hours.
  • Damage to your car: ideally within 48 hours.
  • Batteries, tyres and tint: within 30 days, or within the provider's warranty period if longer.

The provider will redo the service free of charge within 48 hours. If a redo is not possible, is refused or does not fix the problem, you receive a full refund. If the service damaged your car, the provider is responsible for repairing it or compensating you, and Autoflex will help you reach a resolution.

Batteries, tyres and tint film carry the warranty stated by the provider in the app.
